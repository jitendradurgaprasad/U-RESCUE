1. DEM (Digital Elevation Model) — Correct Understanding

Your understanding is mostly correct.

A DEM is a digital representation of the Earth's surface elevation. Data can be collected using:

Satellites (NASA SRTM, ASTER, etc.)
Aircraft using LiDAR
Drones
Ground surveys
GPS-equipped vehicles and devices

For U Rescue, DEM data will be one of the most important inputs because it helps answer:

Which areas are low-lying?
Where will water naturally flow?
Which areas are at risk during floods?
Which nearby locations are at higher elevation and potentially safer?
