2. GIS — Small Correction

GIS (Geographic Information System) does not automatically contain disaster information.

Think of GIS as a smart geographic database and analysis system.

GIS can store and analyze:

Roads
Rivers
Buildings
Hospitals
Schools
Elevation
Population density
Disaster zones
Weather layers

The disaster information is usually added as separate datasets.

A better definition would be:

GIS is a system used to collect, store, manage, analyze, and visualize geographic data from multiple sources.

For U Rescue, GIS will likely become the central brain that combines all data layers.
